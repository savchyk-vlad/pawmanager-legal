3 Description of Service

PawManager is a business management tool for independent dog walkers and small pet care teams (1–10 staff members). The App provides the following core features:

  • Client & dog profiles — store owner contact info, dog details, temperament tags, medical notes, vet info, and access instructions.
  • Schedule & calendar — create single or recurring walks, view by day or week, track walk status (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
  • Walk logging — start and finish walks with a live timer, log actual duration and post-walk notes.
  • Payment tracking — track unpaid balances per client, mark payments as received, log payment method (Cash, Venmo, Zelle, Check).
  • Push notifications — local reminders for upcoming walks, overdue walks, unpaid balances, and daily/monthly summaries.
  • Monthly reports & PDF export — Pro feature. See earnings summaries, top clients, and export tax-ready PDFs.
  • Recurring walk templates — Pro feature. Set weekly schedules that auto-populate the calendar.
PawManager is not a pet care marketplace. We do not connect pet owners with walkers, process payments on your behalf, or assume any liability for pet care services performed by users of this App.

PawManager uses locally scheduled push notifications via Expo Notifications. No server-side system sends notifications — all reminders are scheduled on your device when a walk is created and cancelled when a walk is deleted or rescheduled. Notification types include:

  • Overdue walk alerts (15 minutes past scheduled start time).
  • Unpaid balance reminders after 7 days — Pro feature.
