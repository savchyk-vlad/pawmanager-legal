Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 1, 2026  ·  Contact: savchik.vl@gmail.com

PawManager is a business management tool for independent dog walkers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. We do not sell your data, run ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Who We Are

PawManager ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application developed and operated as an independent product. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at savchik.vl@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Business information: your business name (optional), default walk rate, and app preferences.
  • Client data: names, phone numbers, addresses, and payment methods of your clients — entered by you for your own business use.
  • Dog profiles: dog names, breeds, ages, weights, photos, temperament notes, vet contact information, and medical or behavioral notes — entered by you.
  • Walk records: scheduled and completed walk data including dates, times, durations, and notes.
  • Payment records: payment amounts, dates, and methods you log within the app.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device type and iOS version, collected by Apple and Expo for crash reporting purposes.
  • Push notification tokens: used solely to deliver walk reminders and app notifications to your device.

We do not use any analytics SDKs or third-party tracking tools in the app. We do not collect location data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and deliver the core features of the app — client management, scheduling, and payment tracking.
  • To send you push notifications for walk reminders, overdue payment alerts, and daily summaries (which you can disable in Settings at any time).
  • To generate PDF reports from your walk and payment history (Pro feature).
  • To manage your subscription via RevenueCat and Apple In-App Purchase.
  • To respond to support requests you send to us by email.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (hosted on AWS us-east-1). Supabase uses Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure your data is completely isolated from other users — no other PawManager user can access your clients, walks, or payment records.

Dog photos are stored in Supabase Storage with private bucket policies. All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.

We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account. We cannot recover data if you lose access to your account credentials.

5. Third-Party Services

PawManager uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple — App Store, In-App Purchase, and Apple Sign In. apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Expo / EAS — app build and push notification infrastructure. expo.dev/privacy

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties.

6. Client Data You Enter

You may enter personal information about your clients (names, addresses, phone numbers) into PawManager to manage your business. This data belongs to you. We process it only on your behalf to deliver the app's features. You are responsible for ensuring you have appropriate consent from your clients to store their information in a third-party app.

7. Children's Privacy

PawManager is intended for use by adults (18+) running a professional dog walking business. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us at savchik.vl@gmail.com and we will delete the account promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data directly within the app's Settings.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by emailing savchik.vl@gmail.com.
  • Export your data — Pro users can export monthly reports as PDF.
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time in iOS Settings or within the app.

Upon account deletion request, we will permanently delete your account, all client profiles, dog profiles, walk records, and payment records within 30 days.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all data is permanently deleted within 30 days. Subscription transaction records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat per their own retention policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you via a push notification or in-app banner. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: savchik.vl@gmail.com
